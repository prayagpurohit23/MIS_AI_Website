Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

In May 2017, the WannaCry ransomware spread across the world, locking files on more than 200,000 computers in about 150 countries and demanding payment in Bitcoin. It spread quickly by exploiting a weakness in older, unpatched versions of Microsoft Windows, which disrupted hospitals in the United Kingdom, along with businesses and government offices. The attack was significant in cybersecurity history because it showed how one unpatched vulnerability could shut down critical services, and it pushed organizations to apply security updates faster and to keep reliable backups.
